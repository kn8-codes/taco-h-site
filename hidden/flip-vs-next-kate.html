FLIP vs. NEXT

General liability insurance for Taco H LLC · written for a quick read on your phone · September 25, 2026

2-minute version

Both are legitimate, both cover the same core risk (someone hurt or property damaged because of your business), and both cost roughly $21–$34/month for liability alone. The differences that actually matter for a single-truck startup are:

  1. the food-trailer endorsement (FLIP has one, NEXT doesn't advertise one),
  2. per-incident liability limits,
  3. insurance-company size and backing,
  4. and real customer-service track record.

One honest reminder: neither liability policy covers your truck, your employees, or your own equipment — those are separate policies. This page compares the liability layer, which is the piece you're choosing between now.

The two options

OPTION A · FLIP

FLIP — Food Liability Insurance Program

Food-business-only program run by Veracity Insurance (Utah), around since 2009. Sells only to food trucks, trailers, caterers, and vendors.

Advertised price$25.92/mo or $299/yr (annual saves ~$12)
Limits$1M per incident / $2M per year, product liability included, $0 deductible
Standout featureFood-trailer endorsement (~$12.50/mo) — covers liability around your trailer when parked & detached at venues; no advertised equivalent at NEXT
Extra insuredsFree, unlimited (events love this)
Day-to-dayOnline dashboard, human agents weekdays (Utah hours), COI instantly
Watch-outsThin independent review trail; check the actual insurer on your policy; auto-renews by default
OPTION B · NEXT

NEXT — now ERGO NEXT Insurance

Large digital small-business insurer (1,300+ professions), backed by Munich Re's ERGO Group. Food trucks are one industry among many.

Typical price$21–$33/mo (about 2/3 of food-truck customers land here)
Limits$500K–$1M per incident (their top tier is $1M — no $2M-per-incident option), up to $2M/yr, $0 deductible
Product liabilityIncluded in GL (food-illness claims covered)
Day-to-dayMature mobile app — manage policy, instant certificates, cancel anytime
Watch-outsSupport waits and AI-chat loops are the #1 complaint theme; solo operators often pay more; $1M per-incident ceiling

What's actually different for us

If we run from a tow-behind trailer that parks and detaches at venues: FLIP is the more defensible pick. The trailer endorsement (~$12.50/mo) closes a real gap — incidents around a parked trailer are exactly what ordinary GL policies argue about (FLIP's own data: 28% of claims are auto/trailer-related). A realistic FLIP stack runs about $46–$47/mo (liability + trailer + tools).
If we run a self-propelled truck (no tow-behind trailer) and want one app plus a bigger insurer behind us: NEXT is the more defensible pick — strong app, Munich Re backing, easy bundling later. Just budget for app-first support and confirm the $1M per-incident limit is enough for your venues.

The one thing to check before either choice

Our two quotes were ~$23 and ~$33. NEXT's verified range brackets both; the ~$23 figure sits below FLIP's published floor, so let's confirm which company each quote actually came from before writing the check. And run both quotes at identical limits ($1M/$2M) with the same add-ons so we're comparing like-for-like.

Red flags the marketing pages skip

FLIP

  • Almost no independent reviews for its size — 1 Trustpilot review; their 4.8/5 Google score is self-published.
  • Self-reported customer numbers don't add up (20K / 35K / 50K on different pages).
  • Auto-renews by default — you can be charged annually at renewal unless switched off.
  • You're buying from a program, not one company — check the actual insurer on the policy before paying.

NEXT

  • Support waits are the top complaint — AI chat loops, long queues, audits that take weeks.
  • $1M per-incident ceiling — if an Akron festival contract ever demands $2M per incident, NEXT can't write it.
  • "As low as $25/mo" is a Texas-based minimum, not an Ohio price.
  • Workers'-comp premium audits have generated disputes — relevant only once we add employees.

This week (three small steps)

  1. Pull two identical quotes — FLIP and NEXT, same limits ($1M/$2M), same add-ons. If we use a trailer, ask NEXT directly how they cover liability around it when parked and detached.
  2. Confirm the insurer names — on the FLIP quote, who is the carrier on the declaration page? On NEXT, confirm coverage starts when we need it.
  3. Ask both about renewal — auto-renew? price changes? free additional insureds for our commissary/event hosts?
